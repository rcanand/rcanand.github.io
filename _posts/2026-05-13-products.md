Python, LLM, MLAI LLC, Gumroad, Kindle.

[Gumroad Store](https://rcanand.gumroad.com) - [Kindle](https://www.amazon.com/stores/R.-C.-Anand/author/B0DNY6PYPW) - [Case Studies](https://rcanand.hashnode.dev/)
