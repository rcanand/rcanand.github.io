## If "in the US" means you're buying from outside the US

- **Ticker:** GOOGL (Class A, voting) and GOOG (Class C, non-voting) are economically near-identical; same dividend, same economics.
- **Dividend withholding:** the $0.88/yr dividend (0.31% yield) is negligible either way, but US withholding is 30% unless your country's treaty rate applies — file a **W‑8BEN** with the broker.
- **US estate tax (the real trap):** a non-resident, non-citizen individual gets only a **$60,000 unified credit** against US-situs assets, so holding more than roughly $60–80k of GOOGL directly can expose the excess to US estate tax at up to 40% on death. Many non-US investors use an Irish-domiciled UCITS ETF (CSPX/CNDX) or a local wrapper instead. Check this with a tax adviser in your jurisdiction before sizing.
- **Also:** FX exposure (USD vs your currency) and, depending on where you live, local reporting rules on foreign holdings (e.g., Indian LRS limits and TCS on remittances). If you're a US taxpayer, none of this applies — GOOGL is a US C-corp, dividends are qualified, and there's no withholding.
