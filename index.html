03

Ahead of the Curve

Built before mainstream

A pattern across two decades: independently arriving at ideas that later became widely adopted. Not always first to be famous, but often first to ship.

No-code before "no-code"

Meon · 2015

A fully-hosted, build-anything-without-code web platform - shipped with 25 product demos - years before Webflow and Bubble propelled no-code into the mainstream wave.

Visual knowledge graphs

Deeppage & Thouwords

Multimodal knowledge-graph layers over web content - the same pattern Google later popularized for entities like movies, places, and people in search results.

AI-generated illustrated books

Pikodo → Haixu

Comics-from-Wikipedia (Pikodo, pre-LLM) and 68+ AI-illustrated visual guides plus 9 Kindle books (Haixu, 2024) - shipped before AI-generated illustrated books became a Gumroad and Amazon Kindle category.

Multi-LLM councils

"Social network for AIs"

Built a private experiment where multiple local models picked tasks from diverse domains, debated, judged each other, and scored outputs - the same core pattern that Karpathy's open-source LLM Council (Nov 2025) later made famous.

AI second brains in Obsidian

Claude Code × Obsidian

Heavy Claude Code workflows against personal Obsidian vaults - generating master indexes, concept maps, linked notes, and personalized just-in-time interfaces - before Karpathy's LLM Wiki (Apr 2026) crystallized the pattern.

Now

Maibook · 2026

"Your third brain" - an ongoing local AI feed of personalized agents that expand on your activity. The current chapter, building on every pattern above. maibook.app →
