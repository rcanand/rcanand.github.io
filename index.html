Daily meditation, Cal-Newport-style deep work blocks, regular walks. The older I get the less I'm willing to fragment my attention for anyone.

Health

Following Topol & Johnson

Eric Topol on AI in medicine and longevity biomarkers; Bryan Johnson on the operational side. Tracking what's actionable vs. what's theatre, with sleep and strength as the non-negotiables.
